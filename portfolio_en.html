My Interests and Hobbies

ALPINE SKIING

Alpine skiing has been a passion of mine since a very young age. Each descent represents a new balance between technical mastery, speed, and personal challenge. I particularly enjoy the fluidity of the glide, the intensity of the turns, and the serenity of snow-covered landscapes. More than just a sport, skiing embodies both camaraderie and moments of personal renewal. Whatever path my life takes, it will remain an essential part of who I am.

FISHING IN MOUNTAIN STREAMS

My father introduced me to mountain fishing at an early age, an activity that taught me patience, discipline, and respect for nature. These cherished moments helped me develop an appreciation for the serenity of wide-open spaces and the ability to focus fully on a task in a calm and demanding environment.

PHOTOGRAPHY

I have been practicing photography as an amateur since the age of 11 and have never stopped. Whether capturing landscapes, everyday scenes, or portraits, photography has become a true passion. It allows me to see the world from a different perspective and to preserve unique moments.
